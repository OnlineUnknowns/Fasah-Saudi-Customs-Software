Designed to keep your data on your PC

Fasah Clearance Platform follows a local-first design. Your accounts, declarations and documents are handled on your own computer.

Credentials are protected with Windows DPAPI and AES-256-GCM encryption, and the app is designed not to send your data to third parties by itself.

Alert channels you turn on, such as Telegram or Email, deliver messages through those services.

  • Encrypted credential vault

    DPAPI and AES-256-GCM protect stored credentials.

  • Local-first design

    The application runs on your PC, not on a hosted service.

  • Privacy by design

    No tracking or third-party data sharing is built into the app.

  • Rate-limit protection

    Retry-After handling, exponential backoff and jitter help keep requests within platform limits.
